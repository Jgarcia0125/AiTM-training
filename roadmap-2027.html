ADDENDUM · 2027 PRODUCTION ROADMAP

From prototype to production.

A quarter-by-quarter plan for productionizing SHADOWPROOF at SSFCU
Prepared by: Juan Garcia · InfoSec Contingent on: Risk sponsorship + resource allocation Target GA: Q4 2027
This roadmap assumes sponsorship secured by end of Q4 2026 and formal project kickoff in Q1 2027. It is a proposal, not a commitment — each phase gate would require sign-off from Risk, InfoSec leadership, HR, and L&D before proceeding to the next. Timelines are aggressive but achievable with a single dedicated engineer (myself) plus part-time L&D and IT support.

Milestones at a glance

Q1 2027
Foundation
Requirements, architecture, Azure resources provisioned
Q2 2027
Build
SSO, backend, assessment engine, first 4 modules re-hosted
Q3 2027
Pilot
All 16 modules live, InfoSec + one business unit pilot cohort
Q4 2027
GA
Enterprise rollout, reporting live, phishing sim correlation

Quarterly detail

Q1 · 2027 Foundation & approvals

Workstream

  • Formal requirements gathering with L&D + HR
  • InfoSec architecture review + threat model
  • Data governance sign-off from Legal/HR
  • Azure subscription + resource group provisioning
  • Entra ID app registration for SSO
  • Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA) audit of prototype

Exit Criteria

  • Signed project charter
  • Approved architecture diagram
  • Data governance policy documented
  • Success metrics agreed (see below)
  • Phase gate review with Risk sponsor
Q2 · 2027 Platform build

Workstream

  • Azure App Service deployment (dev + prod)
  • Entra SSO integration (OIDC)
  • Backend API + database (progress, assessments, badges)
  • Assessment engine (question bank per module)
  • First 4 modules re-hosted with SSO + tracking
  • Admin reporting stub (Power BI or embedded)

Exit Criteria

  • End-to-end SSO flow working
  • Assessment + badge issuance functional
  • Penetration test of platform passed
  • Internal alpha with InfoSec team (~15 users)
  • Content accessibility revalidated
Q3 · 2027 Pilot & measure

Workstream

  • Remaining 12 modules migrated + updated
  • Reporting dashboard live for L&D + InfoSec
  • Pilot cohort: InfoSec + one business unit (~50–100 users)
  • Phishing sim campaign pre/post pilot
  • Feedback collection + module iteration
  • Documentation for content maintenance

Exit Criteria

  • Pilot completion rate ≥ 80%
  • Measurable delta in phishing sim click rate
  • Net Promoter Score from pilot cohort
  • Content governance model in place
  • Go/no-go decision on enterprise rollout
Q4 · 2027 Enterprise rollout

Workstream

  • Phased enterprise rollout coordinated with HR
  • Integration with annual training compliance
  • Manager-level reporting for team completion
  • Ongoing content refresh cadence established
  • 2028 module roadmap defined

Exit Criteria

  • 100% of in-scope employees enrolled
  • Reporting integrated with compliance workflow
  • Steady-state operations documented
  • Year-1 ROI assessment scheduled for Q1 2028

Cross-team dependencies

RiskExecutive sponsorship, phase-gate reviews, success metric approval
InfoSecArchitecture review, pentest, content technical accuracy
HR + L&DCurriculum fit, compliance integration, employee comms
LegalData governance, retention, PII handling review
IT / CloudAzure provisioning, Entra registration, ongoing support
CommunicationsLaunch messaging, employee onboarding materials

Proposed success metrics

EngagementModule completion rate; time-in-module vs. skip rate
RetentionKnowledge check pass rates; 90-day recheck scores
BehaviorPhishing simulation click rate delta (pre / post training)
ReportingSuspicious email report rate from trained cohort
Note on scope. This roadmap covers building the platform and content. It does not include ongoing operational costs (Azure infrastructure, my time as content owner in steady state) or the decision on whether SHADOWPROOF supplements or replaces our current awareness vendor — both would need separate discussions with Finance and L&D respectively during Q1 2027.
